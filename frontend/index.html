🎯 Live Hackathon Demonstration Guide

Open Side-by-Side View

1. One-Tap Dose Sync

Click "I TOOK MY MEDICINE" on the senior screen. Hear the musical chime, view the calming green message, and watch the caregiver dashboard immediately record the dose.

2. Multi-Language Audio

Switch language to తెలుగు (Telugu) or हिन्दी (Hindi). Click "🔊 Read Aloud" to hear instructions spoken via Web Speech Synthesis.

3. Missed Dose Escalation

On the Caregiver Demo Bar, click "⚠️ Simulate 30-Min Missed Escalation" to trigger the overdue dose escalation alert and caregiver feed warning.

4. Inventory & Refill Alert

Observe Amlodipine highlighted in red (≤ 5 pills). Click "+ Refill" to instantly restock 30 pills and dismiss the alert.
